## Training Data

Pre-trained on 20.08M decontaminated tokens across 8 domains (en, code, ur, ur-Latn,
ar, hi, es, fr) packaged as `L+1` causal uint16 shards (`2049`-length sequences).
Shards published to `ahmadrrrtx/xrfm-native-shards-v1` (commit `1652affc...`).

All shards passed 13-gram contamination checks against frozen eval benchmarks.

## Evaluation

Strict monotonic validation BPB decrease across all 8 domains on both variants.
See `docs/experiments/track_a_native_training_v1.json` for per-domain BPB curves.

## Intended Use

Research preview only. Track A (`XRFM Native`) is a small-scale native pretraining
exploration (25M–86M parameters). Not intended for production deployment without
further safety evaluation.

## Safety & Dual-Use Boundaries

These are base (uninstructed) language models. They may generate harmful, insecure,
or biased content. Downstream applications must apply their own safety layering.
See Track B (`XRFM CoderSec`) for defensive-security fine-tuned variants.
